About Me

I'm a developer getting back into programming. I did some web and system development back in high school, then took a few detours through other careers and an education before deciding to return to it. It turns out this is what I actually want to do.

What draws me in is the creativity - the freedom to keep learning, and the fact that you build things rather than just study them. I'm most interested in projects that turn something difficult into something that works. I'm not there yet, but the motivation is, and I learn fastest around people who push me.

Outside of code I game, talk to friends, and collect vinyl. Music matters to me more than almost anything - I'm not sure how far I'd get without it. I'd like to call myself a film nerd too, but I'm too busy being a gaming one. No reason it can't be both.
